How to Play

🎯

Objective

Force your opponent into a position where they cannot move. The player who can't move loses the round.

➕

Movement

Move your piece one square — up, down, left, or right. No diagonals, no long slides.

❌

Removal

The tile you just left is permanently removed from the board after every move.

⏱️

Timer

Each player has 30 seconds per turn. Run out of time and you lose the round!

�

Winning

Win the majority of rounds to claim victory. Rounds are always odd so there's always a winner.

🤖

AI Mode

Play against the AI which tries to minimize your moves each turn. Can you beat it?
